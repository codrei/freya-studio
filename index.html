Our Story

Born from a home kitchen, baked with heart.

Freya Studio began as a small home-baking dream — a love for cakes that taste as good as they look. Today, it's a cozy café and bakery in Lipa where every pastry is made from scratch and every cup of coffee is poured with care.

Whether you're here for a quiet afternoon, a custom celebration cake, or to book the studio for an intimate gathering — you'll always find a warm welcome and something sweet.
